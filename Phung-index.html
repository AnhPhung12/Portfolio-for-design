About Me

Design with an Engineering Heart

I bridge the gap between aesthetic vision and logical engineering. My journey began with a fascination for crafting interfaces and matured into a Software Engineering focus at the University of Greenwich. I don't just 'draw' screens; I architect systems.


With a deep understanding of full-stack development (React, SQL, Python), I ensure that every design choice is robust, accessible, and technically feasible. I focus on high-performance UI/UX that solves real-world challenges.


Goal & Personal Interests

I am driven by creating impactful digital products in Fintech and Healthcare. When I'm not designing, I'm exploring Astronomy or styling my K-Streetwear inspired looks. I believe that a great designer is a lifelong learner who understands both data and human psychology.
